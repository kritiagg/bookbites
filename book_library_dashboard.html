🧩 The unignorable-story framework
🎣
1 · Hook in 3 seconds
Open on the most surprising or visual beat — a curiosity gap, a bold claim, a pattern interrupt. The first frame / first line / thumbnail IS the product until they open it.
Hook Point, Made to Stick
👤
2 · One human, real stakes
Every story is one person at one moment of change. Zoom in on the smallest true moment, not the whole epic.
Storyworthy, The Moth
🪜
3 · Put it on a spine
Status quo → disruption → rising tension → transformation. Cut everything that isn't the turn.
Save the Cat, Anatomy of Story, Story
🧲
4 · Make it sticky
Simple · Unexpected · Concrete · Credible · Emotional · Story. Concrete and unexpected beat clever every time.
Made to Stick
📢
5 · Make it shareable
Social currency, triggers, high-arousal emotion, visibility, practical value, story. People share what makes them look good.
Contagious
😆
6 · Find the game
Spot the first unusual thing, label it, and heighten it again and again. Specific + surprising + true = funny.
Truth in Comedy, The Humor Code
🎯
7 · Land the turn
End on a button that recontextualizes everything — the 'so what' punch. The last line is what gets the share/comment.
Comedy Bible, Born Standing Up
